## Implementation plan

Status: done
Current task: 

**Design:** a People section beside Box, Import and Jobs, built on the existing worker methods
(`people.list/unresolved/link/rename/merge`). Linking, renaming and merging change the master at once and
the worker queues a *refresh* job that moves and redraws the affected pages; the page follows that job and
says when the pages are up to date.

- **The queue:** every handle no one is named for (service, identifier, display name, message count,
  busiest first), each with a name field that offers the people already in the box (typing a new name makes
  a person). If the number already names someone else, the page says who and offers to give it to this
  person instead (`move`).
- **People:** each person with their items and names; rename in place; merge into another person after an
  explicit confirmation that says what will happen; the owner can be renamed and merged into, never merged away.
- **Read-only:** the store now learns `read_only` from `worker.info`; every change control is disabled with a
  note, as are imports and box creation.
- **Import report:** "handles still need a name" gets a button to this section.
- **Names to confirm with the maintainer:** the section name "People"; the wording of the merge confirmation.

## Completion report

- **Outcome:** A People section: the queue of handles no one is named for (service, number or name,
  message count, busiest first) with a name field that offers the people already in the box (a new
  name makes a person; a number that already names someone else says so and offers to give it to this
  person instead); the people with their items and names; rename in place; merge into another person
  only after a confirmation that says what will happen; the owner can be renamed and merged into but
  never merged away; every control is disabled, with a note, when the box is read-only. After a change
  the page follows the worker's refresh job ("Redrawing the pages…" then "The pages are up to date.").
  The import report's "handles still need a name" now links here. The store learns `read_only` from
  `worker.info`, and routine page-redraw jobs raise no toast (only a failure does).
- **Verified in a real browser against the real DSH and worker:** after importing WhatsApp and iMessage
  fixtures, naming a WhatsApp handle moves its page to the new person's folder and removes the old one;
  naming an iMessage number creates a second person with their own pages; renaming moves the pages;
  merging combines both people's pages under one name and removes the other; the queue and the people
  list update.
- **Files:** `harness/src/client/pages/PeoplePage.tsx`, `store.ts`, `api.ts`, `locales.ts`,
  `CaixaPage.tsx`, `pages/ImportPage.tsx`, `Toasts.tsx`, `harness/tests/*` (people, store, e2e).
- **Errors:** the first end-to-end run raced on the previous change's "pages are up to date" banner
  (it stays until the next change replaces it): the test now waits for each change's own message first;
  the e2e support now builds the plugin before linking it (linking does not rebuild, so a stale build
  would have hidden UI changes).
- **Verify:** `pnpm --dir harness test` all passing (jsdom tests for the section; the browser test);
  `check-types` clean; Python suite, black and mypy clean.

## Review

Reviewed in-session. Accepted: the status banner of a finished change stays until the next change (a
deliberate, simple behaviour; it could fade); the merge confirmation uses one select rather than a
search box, which is enough for the handful of people a family box holds but would not scale to
hundreds; a handle shown in the queue is identified by (source, identifier), so two identical rows from
different sources are separate on purpose; no automatic suggestion of who a handle probably is (a later
feature). No blockers.
