## Implementation plan

Status: in-progress
Current task: T02

**Design:** a People section beside Box, Import and Jobs, built on the existing worker methods
(`people.list/unresolved/link/rename/merge`). Linking, renaming and merging change the master at once and
the worker queues a *refresh* job that moves and redraws the affected pages; the page follows that job and
says when the pages are up to date.

- **The queue:** every handle no one is named for (service, identifier, display name, message count,
  busiest first), each with a name field that offers the people already in the box (typing a new name makes
  a person). If the number already names someone else, the page says who and offers to give it to this
  person instead (`move`).
- **People:** each person with their items and names; rename in place; merge into another person after an
  explicit confirmation that says what will happen; the owner can be renamed and merged into, never merged away.
- **Read-only:** the store now learns `read_only` from `worker.info`; every change control is disabled with a
  note, as are imports and box creation.
- **Import report:** "handles still need a name" gets a button to this section.
- **Names to confirm with the maintainer:** the section name "People"; the wording of the merge confirmation.
