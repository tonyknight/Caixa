# Phase 1.5 - PDF Completion

**Draft, for the maintainer's review.** The board is complete and every suite passes in the
development container. Nothing has run on the Mac yet, so this report stays a draft until the
trial in *Testing guide* is done and its hot fixes are in.

## Summary

Phase 1.5 - PDF makes documents a domain of the box, starting with PDF. A PDF, a folder or a zip
comes in without its originals being touched. Every archived copy gets a validated, searchable
text layer, and is named and filed by date and author from evidence a local model and the file
name agree on. Documents are found again through search, Ask, and card pages that open the PDF
over the page.

## Scope

The maintainer's draft had seven phases. This project is phases 1–6:

1. import and archiving;
2. OCR and validation;
3. names and folders;
4. reading by a local model;
5. pages, search and review;
6. meaning, Ask and people.

Phase 7, financial transactions from statements and bills, went to the backlog, as agreed.

Decisions made in the design conversation, all recorded in the Requirements:

- Folders are `<year>/<author>`; category and type are views, not folders.
- Names are `(date) Title - Author.pdf`, with no tags, and are cut after 110 characters at a word.
  The date is as precise as it is known.
- `Documents/PDF/Unrecognized/` is at the root of the PDF folder, for documents whose author or
  date can't be worked out.
- A model's proposal is applied when Caixa finds it confident, and goes to review otherwise.
- The PDF library is PyMuPDF (AGPL-3.0, stated in NOTICE). OCR is OCRmyPDF with Tesseract, in
  English by default.
- A document opens in an overlay holding the browser's own PDF viewer.
- Every record is in the master database.
- The collector is `caixa/collectors/pdfImport.py`, so later formats get collectors of their own.
- Author pages are named after the author alone: `<year>/<author>/<author>.html`.
- In authoring mode, an **Author** field, a **Date** field and **Apply** file an Unrecognized
  document.
- Tags come from the model's reading, from a starter set when it fits; the owner can add and
  remove them.

## What was built

**Import (tickets 01–09):**

- A synthetic family of PDFs with known answers (`tests/fixtures/pdf_family.py`), in English and
  Portuguese: digital, scanned, mixed, old OCR, broken text, locked, damaged, duplicates, and
  misleading file names.
- The archival naming convention, `docs/Archival names.md` with `caixa/naming.py`. Every example
  in the document is a test.
- Migration 6 (and 7) in the master: documents, sources, pages, fields with their evidence, tags,
  authors and aliases, runs, the full-text index, and `chunks`.
- The `pdf` collector: preview, hashing and duplicates, archiving, undo, running again, and the
  wizard card.
- Inspection page by page, then OCR of scanned pages only. A broken-text page is read by OCR from
  a render and left unchanged.
- Validation of every copy: two independent readers (PyMuPDF and pikepdf), the same pages, sizes
  and rotation, and the same look page by page. A copy that fails is filed unchanged and can be
  retried (`caixa documents reprocess`).
- Evidence from the file name and the PDF's properties, so filing works without a model.

**Reading (tickets 10–14):**

- The summaries' provider (Apple Intelligence first, or an endpoint) reads each document. A model
  on the internet is always refused.
- JSON schemas for classification and for eight types: statement, check, bill, invoice,
  insurance, tax, legal and correspondence.
- Every quote is verified against its page.
- Reconciliation, conflicts and confidence are decided by Caixa's rules, not the model's own
  score. A confident document is renamed and moved (and undo puts it back); anything else waits
  in **To review** with its reasons.
- Summaries and tags.
- A documents task in `caixa benchmark`.

**Pages and the window (tickets 15–19):**

- PDFs served inline under their own policy, and the overlay, which opens at a page.
- A page of cards per year and author, and the Unrecognized page.
- `Documents/PDF/PDF.html`: a calendar, a year range, author tiles, Unrecognized, To review, and
  a filter. It is linked from the box's index through a generic Chattr slot.
- Search in the served view and on the command line.
- Review and correction from the cards, batched behind **Update the pages**.

**Meaning, Ask and people (tickets 20–22):**

- Passages and embeddings from an endpoint's embedding model, and hybrid search by reciprocal
  rank fusion.
- Ask: answers from retrieved passages, each citation checked; an answer citing anything else is
  refused, and every answer is marked as a model's.
- Authors and people named, linked to the box's people in `item_people`, and listed on their
  person pages through a second generic Chattr slot.

**Documentation and privacy (ticket 23):**

- README, NOTICE (PyMuPDF's AGPL and the OCR tools' licences), and a *PDF documents* section in
  `docs/Documentation/Collector.md`.
- A test that the whole path leaves no page text in any log.
- Owner-only working copies.

**Commands and methods:**

- `caixa import pdf`;
- `caixa documents list | show | search | ask | review | update | tags | authors | enrich |
  embed | reprocess`;
- the worker's `documents.*` methods;
- Settings, Documents: reading, OCR, its languages, and the embedding model;
- `caixa doctor` reports OCRmyPDF, Tesseract with its languages, and Ghostscript.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Owner-only working copies** were in the Requirements (D26) but were not built until ticket 23,
  when writing the documentation showed the gap. `.caixa/work/documents/` is now 0700, with a
  test.
- **Apple Intelligence's guided generation and Apple's embeddings** are not wired into the Swift
  helper. Apple gets the schema in its instructions, and search by meaning needs an endpoint's
  embedding model. Both need the Mac.
- **Questions match any of their words.** Full text asks for every word, so a natural question
  matched nothing; Ask searches with the question's content words joined by OR.
- **The end-to-end Ask test** routes `documents.ask` to a fixed answer in the browser, because
  the stand-in model is not running beside `caixa serve`. The method itself is tested against the
  stand-in in Python.
- **Migration 7** (`document_fields.verified`) was added after migration 6, while reconciliation
  was being built.

## Issues along the way

| Issue | Cause | What we did |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Git's identity was "Claude" | A container restart reset it | Set to the maintainer in the repository's own configuration before the first commit, and checked after every restart. |
| MuPDF opened the damaged fixture | MuPDF repairs it into an empty page instead of refusing it | The test checks for an unreadable document, not a refusal. |
| pikepdf had no `check` | pikepdf 10 names it `check_pdf_syntax` | Use it when present; errors fail, warnings are noted. |
| Undo could not restore a file OCR had replaced in place | Undo followed moves, not rewrites | `undo.keep_file` keeps the earlier copy. |
| A failing model made each document wait out its retries (84 s in a test) | Retries were per document | Reading stops after two failures in a row; the rest wait. |
| A review title was empty | No title yet for a document in Unrecognized | Falls back to the file name. |
| Author pages showed their forms from disk | A `[hidden]` rule was missing | Added. |
| A search could wait two minutes on a stalled model | The embedding timeout was meant for batches | A search waits 10 seconds, then searches by words alone. |
| Reprocessing left stale passages | Page text changed but its passages stayed | New page text drops a document's passages, so they are embedded again. |
| A natural question found nothing | Full text asks for every word | Questions match any of their content words. |
| A commit carried a mypy error | `tail` hid mypy's exit status | Fixed, and the unpushed commit amended; mypy is now read for its count. |

Smaller test-expectation fixes are in each ticket's completion report.

## Known limitations

- Everything ran only on synthetic PDFs, in Linux and Chromium.
- A box person with no messages has no person page, so their documents show only on the document
  pages. This waits for the person page redesign.
- Person pages are matched by title. A Chattr page titled differently from the box person (an
  unresolved handle) shows nothing until the person is matched.
- A citation written as `[1, 2]` is not read as two; such an answer is retried once, then
  refused.
- Search by meaning reads every vector from the database on each search: fast enough for a family
  archive, and worth an index in Stage 2.
- Only the documents' working folder is owner-only. Other collectors' folders keep their earlier
  permissions.
- The licence notes in NOTICE describe the libraries' published licences; they are not legal
  advice.

## Testing guide

Run the suites above and confirm they pass. Then try the project on the Mac, the target, where
nothing of it has run yet.

**Set up (once):**

```bash
brew install ocrmypdf tesseract-lang     # OCRmyPDF, Tesseract, Ghostscript, and the languages
pip install -e tools/chattr -e ".[ocr]"
caixa doctor                             # OCRmyPDF, Tesseract (with por), Ghostscript found
```

In Settings, Documents, set the languages to `eng+por`. Choose a model in Settings, Summaries:
Apple Intelligence first. To try search by meaning, use an LM Studio endpoint with an embedding
model loaded, and name that model in Settings, Documents.

**Try in order, with a test box first and then real documents:**

1. **Import:** Import ▸ PDF documents, then a folder of a few dozen real PDFs: statements, bills,
   letters, and some scans. Check that the preview's counts match the folder, and that the import
   leaves the originals untouched (their modification times are unchanged).
2. **Filing:** open `Documents/PDF/PDF.html` from the box's index. Are the years, authors and
   names right? What is in Unrecognized and To review, and do the reasons make sense?
3. **Reading with Apple Intelligence:** compare a few documents' facts with the PDFs. Run
   `caixa benchmark --tasks documents` to score Apple Intelligence against an endpoint on the
   fixtures. Apple's guided generation (a JSON schema) is not wired into the Swift helper yet,
   so the schema is given in the instructions.
4. **Scans:** a scanned page should look exactly as before and be found by search. A file whose
   OCR failed should be filed unchanged; check that `caixa documents reprocess --failed` tries it
   again.
5. **Safari:** open a card's **Open**, the overlay, at a page (from search) and from disk
   (`file://`). This is the overlay's first try in Safari.
6. **Correct and review:** file an Unrecognized document with Author, Date and Apply. Correct a
   title. Add and remove tags. Rename or merge an author. Then **Update the pages**.
7. **Search and Ask:** search words from a scan; with the embedding model, search a paraphrase.
   Ask a question. Each citation should open the right page, and the answer should say it is a
   model's.
8. **People:** import a person's messages and a letter they wrote. After **Update the pages**,
   their page should list the letter with **Open**.
9. **Privacy:** the job logs in `.caixa/logs/` should hold no page text, and
   `.caixa/work/documents/` should be yours alone (`ls -ld`).

**Report back:** whether Ghostscript was needed at all, how fast OCR and reading were, and any PDF
that was misfiled or refused (its kind, not its contents).

### Each ticket's acceptance criteria, to check by hand

#### 20261124-01: Synthetic PDF fixtures

- [ ] A builder writes the whole family into a folder from a fixed seed; two builds are byte-identical where the format allows (or differ only in documented ways)
- [ ] Every case in *Testing requirements* (Fixtures) is present, in English and Portuguese, synthetic people and invented institutions only
- [ ] A known-answers file describes each fixture: expected page classes, type, title, author, naming date and precision, key fields, filename conflicts, search words
- [ ] Tests check the fixtures are what they claim (page counts, encryption, scanned pages without text, broken text layer garbled)

#### 20261124-02: The archival naming convention

- [ ] `docs/Archival names.md` documents the convention with examples and edge cases, linked from the README
- [ ] `caixa/naming.py` builds `(DATE) Title - Author.ext` with `(YYYY-MM-DD)`, `(YYYY-MM)`, `(YYYY)`; no author omits ` - Author`; no date is not named (Unrecognized keeps the original name)
- [ ] Unsafe characters, control characters, leading/trailing dots and spaces, runs of spaces, capitals and NFC are handled; names are cut after 110 characters at a word
- [ ] A different file of the same name in a folder gets ` (2)`, ` (3)`; the same file is recognised
- [ ] Every example in the document is a test

#### 20261124-03: Migration 6 and document records

- [ ] Migration 6 applies to a new master and to one at version 5; `schema.CURRENT_VERSION` is 6
- [ ] Documents are `items` of type `document`, collector `pdf`, id `pdf:<source sha256>`, with `time_precision` from the naming date
- [ ] A records module upserts a document, its sources, pages (keeping the FTS index in step), fields, tags and runs, and reads them back
- [ ] `documents.list` and `documents.get` service methods and `caixa documents list|show` commands
- [ ] Reset and undo snapshots cover the new tables

#### 20261124-04: The PDF collector

- [ ] Registered as `pdf = "caixa.collectors.pdfImport:collectors"`; appears in `collectors.list` with a how-to card and options
- [ ] Preview counts PDFs, already in the box, duplicates within the source, encrypted, unreadable, pages, size, and writes nothing
- [ ] Run archives each new PDF byte for byte; a source's bytes and modification time are unchanged; a second run adds only source rows
- [ ] Filed into `<YYYY>/<Author>/` when a date and author are known (`caixa.documents.archive.place` takes them; ticket 09 supplies them from the filename and properties), else `Documents/PDF/Unrecognized/` under its original name
- [ ] Undo removes what the import added; progress, cancel and run-again work; up to the Workers setting processes
- [ ] The box README mentions `Documents/`

#### 20261124-05: Inspection page by page

- [ ] Every fixture page gets the class its known answers name
- [ ] Encrypted (password) and damaged files are recognised; an owner-password-only PDF is readable
- [ ] Quality measures and the class are stored per page with the page's own text; preview reports pages likely to need OCR
- [ ] A different file with the same text as a document in the box is flagged `probable_duplicate` for review, never merged

#### 20261124-06: Selective OCR

- [ ] Scanned and mixed fixtures get a text layer on exactly their scanned pages; digital pages are not rasterized; a good OCR layer is kept
- [ ] A broken-text page looks the same and its OCR text is stored as `ocr`
- [ ] Without OCRmyPDF or Tesseract the import succeeds and those pages are `needs_ocr`; `caixa doctor` names what to install
- [ ] Engine, version and settings recorded in `document_runs`

#### 20261124-07: Validation and failure handling

- [ ] Every OCR'd fixture passes; page count, order, sizes, rotation and appearance match within tolerance
- [ ] A forced failure archives the unchanged copy (archived hash = source hash), keeps output and report in `.caixa/work/documents/failed/<id>/`, and puts the document in To review
- [ ] `caixa documents reprocess` (and `documents.reprocess`) succeeds once the cause is gone, without a duplicate record

#### 20261124-08: Page text and full-text index

- [ ] A word printed only on a scanned page finds that document and page
- [ ] Phrases, prefixes, a cheque number, an amount and an account suffix are found
- [ ] Filters by type, category, author, date range, tag, status
- [ ] Accents are ignored in matching

#### 20261124-09: Filename and property evidence

- [ ] Descriptive, uninformative and misleading fixture filenames give the facts their known answers name
- [ ] Properties are read, with producer noise ignored as an author
- [ ] Facts are stored as `document_fields` with source `original_filename` or `pdf_metadata`
- [ ] The import files a document by these when date and author are both known

#### 20261124-10: AI classification

- [ ] With the stand-in endpoint and the stand-in Apple helper, fixtures get proposals of the classification schema
- [ ] Every evidence quote is checked against its page; unverified fields stay proposals
- [ ] A non-local endpoint is refused even with `summaries.allow_remote`
- [ ] Model, provider, prompt version, schema version and time are recorded; the stage can stop and continue

#### 20261124-11: Type schemas

- [ ] Each typed fixture gets its type's fields with evidence and page
- [ ] Amounts are stored as found and as a number parsed by Caixa

#### 20261124-12: Reconciliation, confidence and apply

- [ ] Descriptive fixtures are applied: renamed and moved to `<YYYY>/<Author>/`
- [ ] A misleading filename produces a conflict and To review; no filename fact is dropped without contradicting evidence
- [ ] Date kinds stay separate; re-enriching creates no duplicate and keeps `user_verified`
- [ ] Undo puts moved files back; authors can be renamed and merged with folders following

#### 20261124-13: Summaries and tags

- [ ] Fixtures get summaries within 30–50 words and up to four tags

#### 20261124-16: Author pages and the Unrecognized page

#### 20261124-17: Documents view on the index page

- [ ] The sidebar shows Documents ▸ PDF with its count

#### 20261124-18: Search in the window

- [ ] Searching from the view finds scanned-page words; results open the PDF at the page

  7).

  - merging two different files that hold the same document (they are flagged, not merged).
